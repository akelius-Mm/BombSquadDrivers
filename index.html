How to swing your phone

Wear a wrist strap or grip the phone with both hands, and clear the room. Swing smooth, not hard: the power assist below turns a living-room swing into a full shot.
  1. Turn on Portrait Orientation Lock. Hold the phone in both hands like a club grip, long edge along the shaft and top edge toward your belt buckle. The screen can face you so you can watch the club.
  2. Tap Swing with phone, take your address and hold still. The game reads how you are holding the phone, squares the virtual driver to the target and beeps.
  3. Swing. The driver on screen follows your hands and draws the clubhead's path. Speed sets distance; face and path at impact set the start line and curve.
  4. The yellow trail stays on screen after the shot so you can check your path. The next ball tees up on its own.
