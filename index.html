How to swing your phone

Wear a wrist strap or grip the phone with both hands, and clear the room. Swing smooth, not hard: the power assist below turns a living-room swing into a full shot.
  1. Turn on Portrait Orientation Lock. Hold the phone upright in both hands like a club grip, long edge along the shaft, screen facing you. Either end can point up.
  2. Tap Swing with phone, take your address and hold still. The game reads how you are holding the phone, squares the virtual driver to the target and beeps.
  3. Watch the golfer on screen facing you: their club follows your hands (tap the ⇄ button at the top to flip left and right), and a yellow line draws your clubhead path. Speed sets distance; face and path at impact set the start line and curve.
  4. You can't read the screen mid-swing, so each shot is called out loud. Look back at the screen for the full numbers.
  5. The yellow trail stays on screen after the shot so you can check your path. The next ball tees up on its own.
